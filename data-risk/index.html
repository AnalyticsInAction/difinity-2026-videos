Data risk tiers

Matching AI agent access to the risk of the data · 4-minute narrated explainer · as of November 2026

Interactive version (animation in the browser) Download MP4 (7 MB)

A public price list, a monthly sales table and a patient record need different handling. When you connect an AI agent to a data source it can read whatever that connection allows, quickly and many times over.

What it covers

  • Three tiers: public information, internal business data, sensitive personal information.
  • Step 1: understand what you hold, including fields that identify people in combination. Sensitivity labels travel downstream in Fabric and Power BI.
  • Step 2: decide which agents get access, for what purpose, with what safeguards.
  • Step 3: choose the safeguard: as it is, masked or minimised, synthetic development data, or off limits.
  • Dynamic data masking is not a complete defence on its own: pair it with row and column level security and least privilege.

Sources (checked November 2026): Microsoft Learn: Fabric information protection and sensitivity label inheritance; Fabric Data Warehouse dynamic data masking and security.
